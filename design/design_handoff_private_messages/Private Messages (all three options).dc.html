Telling who said what

Three directions on the same conversation. Each fixes the same two problems: message text sitting at the same weight and colour as its metadata, and no visual difference between the two speakers. Pick one — 1a, 1b, 1c.

1A Paper & ink

Incoming messages arrive as bright paper cards on the dark page; yours stay dark with a hairline outline. Alignment, colour and avatar all say the same thing at once.
